RISK OF RAIN 2

Risk of Rain 2 Wiki

Risk of Rain 2 is a roguelike combat game that has several random elements that make the game feel alive and new every time you do a run. The game has expansive lore and every character has their own abilities to fit different playstyles for each run. You can beat the final boss or continue on, theoretically making the game infinite (at least until it inevitablely crashes)

Favorite Characters

In Risk of Rain 2 there is more than 20 different characters you are able to play. These are my top 3 favorite.

  1. Huntress
    By far my favorite character, Huntress is an archer that relies significantly on her movement to survive, having the ability to teleport while still doing huge damage. Even though she has the lowest base hp of any character, she has some of the highest damage output with the right setup.
  2. Loader
    Everyone loves Loader. I partically like melee characters in games and Loader fits this perfectly, while also having a few additional fun mechanics. She is immune to fall damage and can use a claw to spider man swing around the map, allowing you to do massive damage with your charge punch.
  3. Mercenary
    This was by far the biggest opinion change of a character I've every had in a game. I used to despise mercenary, even though he is a melee character, simply because he felt awkward to play and I always died with him. That was until I figured out the strategy behind him and he quickly became one of my favorite characters, plus he is really cool cause he wields a katana and has some of the most fun mechanics in the game.

Roguelikes?

The roguelike genre is by far by favorite game genre to exist, the mechanics are always very fun and the games are very replayable. A roguelike defines a game that has many randomized aspects to the game that make every run vastly different due to the new items, maps, etc. Every roguelike has their own unique randomized elements, so they don't all feel the same to play.

Risk of Rain 2 DLCs

As of right now, Risk of Rain 2 has three available DLCs, with a fourth one planned to release very soon. Each DLC grants a very large amount of content, where each one has had its flaws but in the end turns out to be really good and fun. However, the DLC content has led to a split in the community, some saying that the new content is amazing and really good while others say it is starting to sway away from the original game they knew and loved.
